# Fixture provenance and use

`corpus.json` and `queries.json` were authored for this course on 18 October 2026. The Rowan simulator, manual revisions, roles, private marker and adversarial attachment are fictional teaching data. No real product manual, personal record or robot operating specification was copied into the fixture.

The original text and question annotations in these two files are dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The marker is deliberately public in the repository; “private” refers to a simulated reader's access policy, not to a real secret. The application code follows the repository's code licensing.

Questions are separated into development and test sets. They are authored cases, not random samples from a real query distribution. Published test failures are now known examples: do not tune to them and report the same results as an independent holdout estimate. Collect new independently written queries for transfer assessment.
