This is the evidence map for [Lectures 25–30](Lecture-25.md). Use it to choose an executable study and to distinguish a research claim from an available artifact. **All rows were checked on 2026-10-13; the cutoff is that date, not 2026-10-31.** This selected register is not an exhaustive literature census or a universal model ranking.

Dates below identify their event: paper submission, announcement, code release, or revision. A moving repository is an access observation, not a reproducibility pin. Before executing a lab, resolve and record its exact code commit, model revision/hash, dataset revision, configuration, normalizer, runtime, and applicable artifact licenses. ## Unresolved provenance and access notes

- **GR00T N1.7:** the inspected repository describes Apache-2.0 general availability, while the inspected Hugging Face card says EA and links NVIDIA Open Model License. Do not silently apply the repository's license statement to every weight revision. Capture the exact packaged terms and resolve the inconsistency for the artifact you use.
- **Physical Intelligence:** the publisher-hosted π0.7, π*0.6, and memory PDFs returned HTTP 403, but their arXiv records were subsequently verified; full arXiv HTML was read for π0.7 and π*0.6. Publisher-download failure does not mean the papers are unavailable. Corresponding public policy weights were not verified.
- **VLAct:** checkpoint configuration and paper configuration can differ. Reproduce the selected artifact's recorded settings; cite the paper only for the experiments it actually reports.
- **Mutable sources:** model cards and repositories may change after this snapshot. Their historical event dates and observed access are recorded here, but immutable copies of every page were not archived. Resolve exact revisions before treating this as an executable bill of materials.
- **Unverified access is not proof of nonexistence.** It means this review did not establish that artifact. An available abstract is sufficient for a dated research pointer, not for asserting weights, code quality, or reproduction success.
