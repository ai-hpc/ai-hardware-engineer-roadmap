# Models, robotics, and ML systems — course review

Reviewed October 14, 2026. **39 course entry pages**: 21 in Phase 3, 5 in robotics, and 13 in ML systems (including one lab index). Each entry received a prerequisite/output/sequence review and a specific transfer exercise. Long guides and lecture collections received targeted checks, not a claim that every command, model claim, or hardware lab was independently reproduced.

Substantive corrections include scalar/PyTorch gradient behavior, image-format bandwidth, Kalibr installation boundaries and geometric assumptions, edge latency claims, MLOps terminology, TF32/INT4/NVFP4 semantics, overlapped timing, scaling efficiency, unsupported quantization speedup targets, and VLA action/control distinctions. The rows below state the reviewed teaching boundary and handoff.
